## Frames and renderer

### splits

New split-frame family: the title zone covers a short column only and a tall column runs from y 36 to the body bottom. Four variants: tall-right, tall-left (tall two-thirds, 558 wide) and tall-right-narrow, tall-left-narrow (tall one-third, 270 wide); standard 18 pt gutter (narrowed from a one-column gutter on owner review).

**PowerPoint:** Add four slide layouts (or a split option on the existing no-rail and nav layouts): header placeholders sized to the short column; a 270-wide short column sets the title at heading size (24/30) with up to three lines (rule at 108/126/162), a 558-wide one keeps title size (rule 108/144/180). The header dot patch starts 36 pt left of the short column (not left of x 57 on nav). Stamp and source line follow the short column.

### nav

Nav-rail tabs are now slots: slide.nav = {items: [2-6 section names], active: index}. Previously the labels were hard-coded (Identify, Define, Build, Optimize) with the second always active. Split frames now combine with the nav rail.

**PowerPoint:** Read tab labels and the active tab from slide.nav; tab height = (body bottom - 36 - 6*(n-1)) / n; active tab Grounded with White text, others Light Gray with Dark Gray text. No template used the nav rail before, so this only affects new templates.

### quadrant-markers

Quadrant chart gains key: "markers": numbered filled circles on the points without the built-in key, for templates with their own legend column.

**PowerPoint:** Support key "markers" on the quadrant chart: draw numbered circles at each point and omit the side key.

### table-checkbox

Table cell type checkbox: an empty outlined box for walk-through checklists (true draws a tick). Distinct from check, which reports status (filled tick or dash).

**PowerPoint:** Support cell type checkbox: 12 pt square, 1 pt Grounded outline, no fill; a tick when true. Used by runbook/step-detail-data and runbook/step-detail-gate.

### new-nodes-2026-10-03

Three new node types: logoslot (logo box, contain-fit image or hatched placeholder), dotmap (dot-matrix US map with office and hub markers from lon/lat) and teamcurve (stacked smooth bands across phases from per-point values).

**PowerPoint:** logoslot: picture with contain fit, or a placeholder rectangle with hatch and label. dotmap: either a pre-rendered dot map picture plus native markers and labels, or native 3 pt squares (many shapes; consider one grouped picture). teamcurve: one freeform shape per band built from the same points with Catmull-Rom tension 0.5 converted to cubic Beziers, so editing the values regenerates the curve; phase dividers are lines, labels are text boxes.

### families-2026-10-03

Template families reorganized: openers split into covers (covers, agendas, dividers, closings) and core (key messages, cards, narrative, quotes); architecture moved out of solution; runbooks moved out of approach; about (firm overview, offerings) moved out of proof. Keys (id/variant) are unchanged.

**PowerPoint:** No layout change. Update any family-based grouping or lookup; keys are stable.

### dotmap-americas-uk

dotmap gains region americas-uk: US, Mexico and Central America to Costa Rica plus a framed Great Britain inset (no Ireland, no break mark, after owner review).

**PowerPoint:** Second map panel set; markers land in the panel containing their lon/lat. Inset frame is a 0.75 pt Medium Gray rectangle.

### teamcurve-phaselabels

teamcurve phaseLabels false / phaseH options (used when phase names sit elsewhere).

**PowerPoint:** Honor phaseLabels false: omit the phase label row under the plot.

### dotmap-geography

Dot maps now use real outlines: US Census cartographic shoreline (us-atlas), Natural Earth 1:50m for Mexico, Central America and Great Britain (world-atlas), both public domain, simplified; the Great Lakes are carved out.

**PowerPoint:** If pptxgengo draws the dot field natively, use the same outline data (or a pre-rendered dot picture generated from it) so the maps match.

Changed keys in `frames/v0/frames.json`: splits.
