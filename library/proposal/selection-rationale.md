## Content and qualification rules

- The 18,000 monthly cases, 22% handoff baseline, and 14% incomplete-intake baseline are synthetic assumptions. The baseline is not independently validated.
- The 12%, 7%, and 95% targets are hypotheses only, not commitments or savings claims.
- All timing is illustrative. Access readiness, agreed acceptance measures, data remediation, vendor constraints, and control changes can affect sequence. Expanded journeys, integrations, or operating hours require separate scope decisions.
- A 21-role organization diagram shows responsibilities and reporting, not 21 full-time people. A 19-tile roster shows role categories, not additive staffing. Allocation, names, availability, rates, and delivery calendar require a separate agreement.
- The biography describes a proposed role and experience categories to validate. It does not describe a real person or actual credential.
- The parallel-process layout has exactly two paths; it is not presented as three pod swimlanes. The three pods belong on the delivery-team page.
- This JSON is narrative guidance only. It does not bind component values or change library contracts. The opening phrase accent requires a dense argument-template enhancement before it can serve as the final showcase opening.
