None of the pairs is visually distinct enough to justify separate layout families from these previews alone. The table, Venn, and timeline pairs contain visible content/style differences; 111–112 appears to differ primarily by palette and should remain two source occurrences linked to one candidate family. Exact native-content equivalence, editability, effective styles, and object/relationship identity still require structural review. All four remain candidates pending curator approval and adaptation tests.
