Sustain: operate, evolve, and finish decommissioning

A living knowledge base reduces dependence on a few experts and gives the next change a stronger starting point.

OPERATE AND EVOLVE

CORE WORK

Establish ownership, service levels, observability, security, cost management, and a product backlog.

Keep process, rule, architecture, decision, test, runbook, and operating knowledge current.

AI CONTRIBUTION

Assist incident triage, impact analysis, documentation, test maintenance, and backlog development using governed context.

OUTPUT

Operating model, product roadmap, and measured service and business performance

DECOMMISSION

CORE WORK

Remove old code, infrastructure, data paths, interfaces, jobs, licenses, support arrangements, and manual controls.

Find remaining dependencies and consumers before retirement decisions.

AI CONTRIBUTION

Trace dependencies and transfer the business, control, data, and support knowledge that must survive.

OUTPUT

Verified retirement, closed controls, ended dual run, and realized savings

Full value requires both improvement and verified retirement.
