```text
A governed evidence base and business process map reduce rediscovery by giving teams and AI agents bounded, source-linked context for each delivery task.
```

## Speaker notes

```text
Unified context is the strategic idea.
A knowledge graph is one possible implementation pattern and is not a claim of a fully delivered product.
Context needs provenance, versioning, permissions, and clear separation of evidence from inference.
Stale context can accelerate the wrong work.
Human review and testing determine correctness.
Transition: Cutover is a high-value use of connected context because planning, preparation, and monitoring depend on business, data, control, and technology evidence.
```
