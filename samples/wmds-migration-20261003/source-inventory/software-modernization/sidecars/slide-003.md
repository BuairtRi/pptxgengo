## Speaker notes

```text
SPEAKER BEATS
The historical choice often looked poor in every direction: continue carrying legacy risk, reorganize the business around a packaged platform, or accept a large custom rebuild.
Paint a future the business wants. Modernization can unify an acquired platform, unlock a market, redesign a consequential process, or create the foundation for agent-enabled work.
“One-tenth of what you expect” is a conditional thought experiment. It invites the audience to reconsider deferred opportunities; it is not a claim that every program now costs one-tenth as much.
The strategic claim is expanded choice. Business-fit modernization can deserve consideration more often.
TRANSITION
The first consequence of changed economics is strategic choice. The business no longer has to assume that its only practical future is to preserve legacy systems or conform to a broad packaged platform.
EVIDENCE / GUARDRAIL
Treat “the script has flipped” as the campaign thesis. Slide 1A explains the strategic implication, and slide 2 shows the workstreams and cases behind it. Do not attach a universal percentage or claim that every modernization is now inexpensive.
```
