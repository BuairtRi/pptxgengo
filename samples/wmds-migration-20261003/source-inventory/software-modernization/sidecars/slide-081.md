RATIONALIZATION & MODERNIZATION STRATEGY

Rationalization connects application decisions to reimagined business workflows, a modernization roadmap, and conceptual architecture

OBJECTIVES

OUTPUTS

01

Prioritize the application portfolio

Confirm in-scope applications and understand costs, roadmaps, ownership, and planned changes

02

Assess value, health, and risk

Assess business value, technical health, risk, dependencies, and architecture fit through owner interviews and technology analysis

03

Reconstruct current-state business processes

Map workflows, handoffs, pain points, exceptions, and application touchpoints with business and application owners

04

Define application dispositions

Create and socialize initial dispositions; identify the applications best suited for modernization

05

Reimagine future-state business workflows

Design workflows tailored to how the business operates, then translate them into modernization targets, architecture, interim states, and a sequenced roadmap

Decision-ready rationalization outputs

Portfolio fact base and current-state workflow map

Business value, technical health, and risk profiles

Application disposition register

Reimagined workflow and modernization roadmap

Conceptual future-state architecture

EXECUTIVE DECISION

Approve strategy  •  Refine scope  •  Begin execution
