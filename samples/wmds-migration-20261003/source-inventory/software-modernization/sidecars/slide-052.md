Modernized a solar interconnection web app from AngularJS to Angular, improving maintainability and efficiency. Re-wrote pages using Agile methods, driving feature parity alongside stakeholder input. Healthcare Professional Association: Life Sciences

Lab & Diagnostic Testing Provider: Life Sciences
