Large Credit Reporting Agency: High Tech & Software

Transformed client's offer management by modernizing their outdated system. Embedded product and development team members to design and build a system that personalizes offers using user demographics. This cut publishing and update times from 6-8 weeks to under 24 hours.

Golf Club Owner/Operator: Consumer & Industrial Products

Large Property & Casualty Insurance Provider: Insurance

Re-platformed the client's legacy, Windows-only applications to a cloud-first tech stack. Unified the customer experience by modernizing workflows and integrated five applications into a single solution. Simplified processes and improved access to support and pricing specials.

Workplace Safety & Compliance Provider: Healthcare

Modernized outdated architecture to reduce risks and enable faster releases. Implemented Agile delivery process. Created .NET Core microservice and Azure-hosted SQL database to decouple occupational health domain. Replacing ASP.NET web pages with React for improved user experiences.
