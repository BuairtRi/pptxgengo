## Speaker notes

```text
SPEAKER BEATS
The technical measures matter only when they connect to a business outcome. These sources support three value pools: meeting an urgent deadline, protecting continuity during a high-scale transition, and lowering recurring operating effort.
ACCELERATE DELIVERY — HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONAL ASSOCIATION
A critical customer-facing application faced an urgent AngularJS end-of-life deadline. West Monroe assessed and rewrote the application within five months, strengthened security, and created repeatable patterns for future enhancements. The business value is deadline certainty and a maintainable base for continued change. The source does not provide project cost, a before-AI duration, or evidence that AI caused the five-month result.
PROTECT CONTINUITY — LARGE INVESTOR-OWNED UTILITY
The client needed to modernize a decade-old solar interconnection solution supporting workflows across six operating companies. The program migrated more than 675,000 lines of code and modified more than 800 files. Business stakeholders validated feature parity, and the old and new interfaces ran side by side in production before final cutover. The value is protection of critical operations, improved maintainability and development efficiency, and reduced transition risk. The source does not report duration, program cost, cost savings, or a before-AI baseline.
LOWER OPERATING EFFORT — HR COMPLIANCE SOFTWARE PROVIDER
The legacy on-premise platform had lengthy report-processing times, limited third-party integration, duplicated functionality, complex workflows, and security vulnerabilities. The redesigned cloud platform used AI/ML-enabled automation to simplify highly manual tasks. The reported result was a 65% reduction in total report-generation duration and more than $30,000 in average savings per customer based on consultant time saved. This is the strongest quantified business-value proof on the slide.
EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE
Do not imply a before-versus-after AI comparison that the sources do not contain. The source material does not provide the cost or timeline of a traditional non-AI alternative. The first two cases demonstrate modernization value pools, not isolated AI causality. Revalidate every metric immediately before release.
```
