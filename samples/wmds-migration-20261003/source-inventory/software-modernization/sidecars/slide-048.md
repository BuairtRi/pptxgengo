## Extracted slide text

```text
From security scan findings to a reviewable remediation plan

INPUTS

Structured CVE or scanner findings  •  relevant source repository and Evolve evidence store  •  component, dependency, integration, and business-criticality context

01

NORMALIZE THE INVENTORY

Organize findings, affected packages or components, severity, scanner evidence, and missing information.

02

CONNECT FINDINGS TO THE SYSTEM

Map candidate issues to source-linked components, dependencies, code paths, integrations, and potential blast radius where evidence supports it.

03

REVIEW APPLICABILITY

Flag supporting evidence, possible duplication, uncertain reachability, compensating controls, and false-positive questions for a security specialist.

04

PRIORITIZE REMEDIATION

Consider severity with business criticality, exposure, dependencies, operational risk, and cost of delay.

05

PREPARE THE PLAN

Produce a reviewable package with evidence links, affected areas, candidate actions, sequencing, validation tests, estimation questions, and owners.

HUMAN CONTROL

Security and engineering specialists confirm applicability, exploitability, remediation, testing, and risk acceptance.

PILOT OR SCAFFOLD

The audited product accepts structured CVE inputs and includes a triage handler and artifact outputs, but the default production runner is unavailable. Position as a pilot until the product owner confirms a production-ready execution path and evidence from real use.
```
