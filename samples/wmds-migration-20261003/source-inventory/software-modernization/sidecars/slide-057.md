Client needed to modernize a decade-old solution that powers workflows for solar interconnection across their six operating companies. The application’s AngularJS front-end technology was nearing end-of-life and slowing down development team efficiency.

LARGE INVESTOR-OWNED UTILITY

How can we accelerate innovation and increase security maturity on our solar interconnection solution?

Modernization Strategy & Build
Assessed leading frameworks and recommended Angular based on key business priorities of maintainability, theming, and dynamic forms. Angular enhances maintainability with its opinionated defaults, facilitating quick project initiation and simplifying new developer onboarding. Additionally, Angular Material and Formly support dynamic theming and form generation, respectively, accelerating front-end development with robust components and JSON-based configurations.
Our team inventoried all existing pages, rationalized which pages should carry over to the new Angular UI, re-wrote them leveraging an Agile development process, and engaged business stakeholders to validate feature parity prior to final cutover.
Conducted pilot phase where the old and new UIs were running side by side in production to enable a seamless transition for the business.
