CLIENT STORIES

Hulu was approaching the launch of Hulu Live TV and sought out West Monroe to guide their development teams and ensure a timely launch for both their iOS app and Apple TV app.

Hulu

Native iOS & Apple TV Design & Build

Modernization Strategy & Build
Our team co-located with the Hulu team in Santa Monica while working with their remote iOS team in Seattle. The two worked together to re-write their iOS app while building out their Apple TV app to support the launch of Hulu Live TV.
As part of the re-write, both apps were updated to use Swift instead of Objective-C. A reactive framework was introduced to better manage internal app state.
A significant update was also made to the iOS and tvOS CI pipelines to provide a faster, automated feedback loop for integration tests.
The team successfully rolled out the app to millions of Apple TV users and was able to make quick and efficient updates.
