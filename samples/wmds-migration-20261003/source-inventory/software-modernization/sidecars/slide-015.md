SOFTWARE MODERNIZATION

With constant AI innovations, software modernization is faster, easier, cheaper, and more necessary than ever before. But most organizations don’t understand how to capitalize on AI’s value in the modernization lifecycle.

Legacy Systems Are Holding the Business Back
Legacy architecture and environments are expensive to maintain, increasingly vulnerable, slowing innovation and time-to-market, and a drag on developer productivity.

MOST MODERNIZATIONS FAIL
60-75%
of software modernization efforts go over budget or exceed timeline, are partially abandoned or scaled back, and/or deliver less business value than promised.

Most Orgs Don’t Know How to Optimize What They Have
The majority of modernization efforts become “lift-and-shift” exercises that replicate the past instead of reimaging systems for AI and rearchitecting them to create future value.
