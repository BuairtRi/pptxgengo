We build together alongside your teams and can scale up or down with both onshore and near-shore talent, based on your needs.We slowly step back over time as we enable your teams to move forward with and sustain your modernization program.

Software Modernization

We meet you wherever you’re at. We often take a lead role in standing up the modern architecture and technology stack aligned to best practices, leaning on our onshore resources for deeper engagement with your team members.

Once the foundation and structure is in place, we begin to step back into a supporting role as your teams take the lead, often augmenting with near-shore resources to scale up and build faster at an attractive price point while coaching and mentoring your team members on the modern platform.

As your team gains a level of comfort and assurance in maintaining the effort, we fade into the background, often moving on to new problems to solve within the organization.

West Monroe

We will fill gaps in your team and take a leadership role in establishing processes, architecture, and technologies—getting the modernization effort off to solid start.

As we continue to partner with you on the modernization roadmap—measuring progress and reprioritizing at each planning horizon—we continually evaluate the balance of assistance required to successfully provide value.

As your teams grow confident in handling modernization efforts, we plan the roll off strategy based on your goals, while you take on long-term sustainment of the modernized software.
