## Preserve the safeguards already implemented

Native sections are available in YAML and through `project section list/add/rename/remove`. They preserve comments and predecessor source, validate ordered anchors/unique groups, atomically update YAML, keep visible divider copy separate from the group title, and retain notes/hidden metadata. The five-page pilot passed actual PowerPoint clean open, native group/notes/hidden inspection and image review. Its README now links the accepted `review/native-powerpoint-review.json` receipt and records the hidden fifth-page inspection.

Chart `preserveCategories:true` and `preserveWorkbookZeros:true` are strict explicit opt-ins. Dental's final cache and actual XLSX cell audits verify exact category casing and six numeric zeros; missing observations remain missing. Team-curve local allocation now reserves stroke ink. These fixes should remain regression requirements, rather than further conversion work.
