Original authored notes
Targets are hypotheses for the week-2 gate. Basis: unplanned events averaged about two a month in 2026 (15 in eight months); critical-path automation pace uses QA's own trend (about 180 tests added in five months, roughly 9 a week) plus the two full-time QE engineers in the Testing & release controls workstream; the hardening window comes from the plan QA leadership already drafted. Natural targets (zero repeats, 100% coverage of credentials, matching builds, signed specs) need no baseline. Delivery-metric targets wait for the week-2 baseline.
