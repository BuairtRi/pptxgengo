Opportunity brief to implementation path

Make the decision path from an internal signal to an implementation plan visible and repeatable.

RECOMMENDATION 03

WHAT SHOULD BE DONE

Standardize the minimum content for an opportunity brief: problem, evidence, outcome, decision, and why now.

Carry the decision logic through product brief or PRD, feature brief, epic, technical design and implementation plan.

Make ownership, acceptance, design context, dependencies, and release evidence explicit before delivery scope is committed.

HOW TO ACTIVATE IT

Select one internal signal from a live initiative and complete the opportunity brief.

Review the brief with Product, Engineering, UX, and the initiative sponsor before creating the PRD.

Trace the same decision through the feature brief, epic, technical design and implementation plan, then record handoff gaps and rework.

Owner: Product + Engineering | Dependency: shared templates, architectural spec template, design context, initiative sponsor | Measure: handoff quality, rework, and decision clarity before commitment
