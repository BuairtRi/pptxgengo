Product management as business ownership

Shift Product accountability toward customer evidence, outcomes, roadmap, adoption, and learning.

RECOMMENDATION 02

WHAT SHOULD BE DONE

Define what Product owns from opportunity through post-launch learning, separate from backlog administration.

Clarify decision rights across Product Manager, Product Owner, Engineering Lead, Sales, and Operations.

Use the opportunity brief and PRD to carry business context and decision logic into delivery.

HOW TO ACTIVATE IT

Map current decision rights and identify one gap that slows the live initiative.

Assign a PM owner for the initiative's outcome and learning loop.

Review adoption, customer evidence, and business results in the same cadence as delivery progress.

Owner: Product leadership | Dependency: role clarity, customer and usage data, outcome measures | Measure: decisions supported by evidence and post-launch learning captured
