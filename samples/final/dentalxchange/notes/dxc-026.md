Outcome-led prioritization

Make priority and commitment decisions from evidence about outcomes, feasibility, and capacity.

RECOMMENDATION 01

WHAT SHOULD BE DONE

Set explicit decision criteria for value, urgency, feasibility, and dependency before commitments are made.

Protect capacity for high-value work, with an explicit allocation for support, patches, and escalations and show a credible 3-4 sprint view.

Require Engineering-informed effort and risk input before customer dates become commitments.

HOW TO ACTIVATE IT

Select one live initiative and apply the criteria to the next prioritization decision.

Compare the committed scope with evidence and capacity, then record the trade-off.

Review predictability, scope changes, and rework in the activation cadence, using flow data where teams have moved off story points.


Owner: Product leadership + Engineering leadership | Dependency: roadmap, customer commitments, capacity data | Measure: commitment predictability, late scope change rate, and share of capacity spent on unplanned work
