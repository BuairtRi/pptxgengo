What we saw

[To be completed by the product team]

Two platforms at very different maturity: Reconcile AI leads on technical design, AI-assisted build, and test automation, while Core relies on manual regression. Half of 2026 Core production changes were unplanned, and delivery is not measured end to end.

What we recommend now

[To be completed by the product team]

Five engineering recommendations, each starting with one pilot: a root-cause review of unplanned changes (E1), a map of Core's highest-risk regression (E2), wait times for access and test data (E3), a delivery baseline (E4), and sanctioned tools for agentic work (E5).

How it rolls out

[To be completed by the product team]

One pilot pod per recommendation: Core for release stability and quality, Reconcile AI and the Rules Engine for environments and data, Reconcile AI for agentic delivery. What works extends to Eligibility AI, Pay Connect, and the other pods, with a light, human-gated version for Core.

What we need from leadership

[To be completed by the product team]

An owner for each of E1–E5, agreement on the pilot pods, the sanctioned AI tools and PHI rule before the December AIDLC rollout, and access to release and Jira data to build the baseline.
