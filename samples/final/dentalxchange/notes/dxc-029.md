RECOMMENDATION 04

WHAT SHOULD BE DONE

Define the minimum cross-functional team and artifact set required before a commitment is ready.

Include Product, UX, Engineering, QA, Release, and Operations in early feasibility and evidence work.

Maintain living documentation in a source of truth that supports handoffs and reuse.

HOW TO ACTIVATE IT

Choose one initiative and run a pre-commitment working session.

Capture the open feasibility, design, release, and operational questions in one shared record.

Measure wait time, rework, and late discovery of issues before and after the test.

Owner: Product + Engineering | Dependency: stakeholder access, cadence, Figma and design context | Measure: rework, wait time, and issues found before commitment
