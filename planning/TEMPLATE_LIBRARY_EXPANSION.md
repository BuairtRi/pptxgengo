Business architecture, technical architecture, product overview, product detail,

## Independent selection dimensions

- Category describes the subject/communication purpose.
- Altitude describes framing, overview, explanation, detail or reference.
- Density describes sparse, medium or dense presentation independently of altitude.
- Visual arrangement describes slots/components and their relationships.
- Sequence describes multiple slides, their role/order and repeated detail rules.

A dense overview and a sparse component detail are both valid. Product overview
and product detail are distinct purposes, not necessarily different native layouts.
Four-phase overview -> one-to-three pages per phase is a sequence contract and
must not count as an extra family for each repeated phase.

- Source occurrences: retain every registered slide, even duplicates/instructions.

The compiler preserves existing canonical families, rejects missing/duplicate source
rows, stale hashes, and missing family definitions. Cross-slice merges need an
explicit reviewed decision with rationale. User design preferences remain separate
from an agent's nomination to the implementation queue.
